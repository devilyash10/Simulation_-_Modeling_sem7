## **1. What is Queuing Theory?**

**Definition:** Queuing Theory is a mathematical method used to study waiting lines, customer arrivals and service processes.

In simple words, it helps us understand why queues form, how long customers wait and how to reduce waiting time.

### **Real-Life Examples 🏦**

- **Bank:** Customers wait for their turn at service counters.
- **Hospital:** Patients wait to consult a doctor.
- **Railway Station:** Passengers wait at ticket counters.
- **Computer Networks:** Data packets wait to be processed.
- **Operating Systems:** Processes wait for CPU execution.

## **2. Basic Components of a Queuing System**

Every queuing system generally has the following components:

### **1. Calling Population**

It refers to the group of people or objects that may arrive for service.

*Example:* All customers who may visit a bank.

### **2. Arrival Process**

It describes how customers or objects enter the system.

*Example:* Customers arriving at a bank every few minutes.

### **3. Waiting Line (Queue)**

It is the line in which customers wait until service becomes available.

*Example:* Customers standing outside a bank counter.

### **4. Service Mechanism**

It refers to the person, machine or facility that provides service.

*Example:* A bank employee serving customers.

### **5. Service Time**

It is the time required to serve one customer.

*Example:* A bank employee takes 5 minutes to serve a customer.

### **6. Queue Discipline**

It defines the order in which customers receive service.

*Example:* First Come, First Served (FCFS), where the customer arriving first is served first.

## **3. Working of a Queuing System**

Let's understand the complete process with a bank example.

**Working of a Queuing System**

**Customers Arrive**

**Wait in Queue**

**Receive Service**

**Leave the System**

**Example:**

Suppose five customers arrive at a bank. If only one employee is available, customers wait for their turn. After receiving service, each customer leaves the system.

Queuing Theory helps calculate the average waiting time, queue length and employee utilization.

## **4. Objectives of Queuing Theory**

1. **Reduce Waiting Time:** Help customers spend less time waiting.
2. **Reduce Queue Length:** Prevent unnecessarily long queues.
3. **Improve Service:** Make the service process faster and more efficient.
4. **Proper Resource Utilization:** Ensure employees and machines are used effectively.
5. **Reduce Operating Cost:** Avoid unnecessary service counters and resources.

## **5. Important Terms in Queuing Theory**

| Term             | Simple Meaning                                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrival Rate (λ) | Average number of customers arriving per unit of time.    |
| Service Rate (μ) | Average number of customers served per unit of time.      |
| Queue Length     | Number of customers waiting in line.                      |
| Waiting Time     | Time spent waiting before service.                        |
| Server           | Person or machine providing service.                      |
| Utilization      | How much of the server's time is spent providing service. |
