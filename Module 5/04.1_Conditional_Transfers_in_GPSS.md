# **Module V – Conditional Transfers in GPSS**

## **Q1. What are Conditional Transfers in GPSS? Explain with an example.**

**Exam Answer (English):**

Conditional Transfer in GPSS is a mechanism that allows a transaction to move from one block to another depending on whether a specified condition is satisfied.

It is used to control the flow of transactions in a simulation model.

**Example:**

In a bank, if Counter 1 is free, the customer is sent to Counter 1. Otherwise, the customer is sent to Counter 2.

### **Importance**

1. Controls the movement of transactions.
2. Helps in decision-making during simulation.
3. Allows different paths based on conditions.
4. Improves resource utilization.
5. Helps model real-world systems.

**Hinglish Summary:**

Conditional Transfer ka matlab hai condition check karke transaction ko alag block ya route par bhejna.

Example: Agar Counter 1 free hai toh customer wahan jayega, otherwise Counter 2 par jayega.

## **Q2. Explain the working of Conditional Transfers in GPSS.**

**Exam Answer (English):**

Conditional Transfers work by checking a particular condition during simulation.

1. A transaction reaches a conditional transfer block.
2. The specified condition is checked.
3. If the condition is true, the transaction follows one path.
4. If the condition is false, the transaction follows another path.
5. The simulation continues according to the selected path.

**Hinglish Summary:**

Pehle condition check hoti hai. Agar condition true hai toh ek path follow hota hai, aur false hone par doosra path follow hota hai.

## **Q3. Give an example of Conditional Transfers in GPSS.**

**Exam Answer (English):**

Consider a bank having two service counters. When a customer arrives, the system checks the availability of Counter 1.

- If Counter 1 is free, the customer is sent to Counter 1.
- Otherwise, the customer is sent to Counter 2.

This decision-based movement of customers is called Conditional Transfer.
